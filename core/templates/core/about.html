Lamar Investment Company Ltd is a professional cleaning services company based in Kigali, committed to delivering high-quality, reliable, and efficient cleaning solutions for commercial, residential, and institutional clients. We pride ourselves on maintaining clean, healthy, and safe environments through trained personnel, modern cleaning practices, and customer-focused service delivery.

Clients bring us in because they need the work done right without having to supervise it. That means arriving on time, following through on the scope agreed, and treating every property — office, home, or facility — with the same level of care.

We're committed to building long-term relationships with our clients through trust, quality service, and continuous improvement — the standard doesn't change as Lamar grows; every new contract is handled with the same reliability that built the ones before it.

Our Vision

To become a leading and trusted cleaning services provider recognized for excellence, reliability, and professionalism.

Our Mission

To provide quality cleaning services that exceed client expectations while promoting cleanliness, hygiene, safety, and environmental responsibility.

Our Core Values

Professionalism

Every team member is trained, briefed on the job, and accountable for the standard of the finished work.

Integrity

We do what we say we'll do, quote honestly, and own it when something needs correcting.

Reliability

Scheduled work happens on schedule. If something changes, you hear about it before it becomes a problem.

Customer Satisfaction

We check in after the job is done — not just before it starts — because satisfaction is measured after the fact.

Teamwork

Cleaners, supervisors, and management work together so the job runs smoothly from scheduling to sign-off.

Accountability

If something falls short of the standard, we hear about it, fix it, and take responsibility — no excuses.

Excellence

We hold every job to the same standard, whether it's a single home visit or a multi-year contract.
