Our Safety Protocol

Four checkpoints every job passes through, from the products we bring to how we close it out.

01

Product safety

Cleaning agents are chosen for the surface and setting they're used on, with attention to spaces used by children, patients, or food handling.

02

Staff protection

Team members are equipped with the protective gear appropriate to the job, from routine cleaning to deep and post-construction work.

03

Site protocols

For institutional and commercial sites, our teams follow the access, timing, and safety rules set by the property — no shortcuts.

04

Incident response

If anything unexpected comes up on site, it's reported to the client directly and addressed before the job is considered complete.
